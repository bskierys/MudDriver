# PLAN — Phase V: Visuals (mud spray + dirty jeep)

**Status:** closed after revision 1 (2026-11-07), runs before Phase 3 River. Next: `/plan-roadmap`.

References in `Concept/`: `jeep-wrangler.png` (clean start), `jeep_wrangler_mud_reference.jpg` (dirt cap), `mud-car-drive.mp4` (spray target, 1.2 s), `mud-car-splash.mp4` (big sheet splash; aspirational, not a target).

## Problem

The jeep now looks like a Wrangler, but mud doesn't read as mud. The current spray (`NS_B1MudSpray`: one GPU sprite emitter, 10–28 cm round clumps, slip-only) looks like a few big brown blobs. In the reference, a spinning or rolling tire throws a dense rooster tail of small dark clumps of mixed sizes, a few bigger chunks, and a faint brown haze behind them. The jeep also stays showroom-clean however long it drives in mud. In the reference, the tires are caked brown first, then the arches and sills, then splatter climbs the doors and bumpers, and finally spots reach the hood, roof and glass.

Who it's for: the player's experience (the prototype should look like the concept), and the user's playtests (mud time becomes visible on the car). This phase also delivers Phase 1's stretch item "dirt mask on the jeep".

**Success in one sentence:** after about 90 s of normal driving through medium mud, the Wrangler looks like `jeep_wrangler_mud_reference.jpg` and stops getting dirtier, and the tires throw a spray the user judges close to `mud-car-drive.mp4`.

## Scope

Work order is look-first. The user checks still images of the dirt look before any accumulation logic is built.

**0. Spikes (each answers one yes/no before the real work)**
- **Niagara:** can `NiagaraToolset_System` build a GPU emitter with a mesh renderer and depth-buffer collision? If not, clumps fall back to sprites with a clump-shaped mask.
- **Custom Primitive Data (CPD):**
  - Can one CPD scalar be set on the inherited C++ tire components and on the `Chassis` of `BP_B1Jeep_Wrangler`, and read in `M_Wrangler_Base`?
  - Are `ComponentTags` editable on those inherited components?
  - Do the Wrangler MIs already use any CPD slots?
- **Texture import:** import one generated PNG with `TextureTools.import_file` and check the compression settings (masks: linear, BC4/BC5).
- **Body space:** read the bounds of `SM_Wrangler_Body` and where the `VisWheel_*` sockets sit in the body mesh's space. This gives the arch centres.

**A. Dirt look (stills first)**
- **Generator:** `Scripts/MudTextures/` (Python + numpy, fixed seeds, parameters in the README, re-runnable):
  - tileable large-blotch mask;
  - tileable small-splatter mask;
  - breakup noise;
  - low-poly clump mesh (OBJ) for the spray.
- **Imports:** dirt textures to `/Game/B1/Mud/Dirt/`, the clump mesh to `/Game/B1/FX/`. These are mud-side assets, so they migrate with `Mud/`.
- **Material function `MF_B1MudDirt`** (`/Game/B1/Mud/Dirt/`), called by `M_Wrangler_Base`, so every `MI_Wrangler_*` inherits it:
  - a two-scale triplanar mask in the mesh's local space (blotch × splatter), stable on a moving body and a rotating tire;
  - a reveal threshold that falls as dirt grows, weighted per region:
    1. **tires** (wheel value): full brown coat;
    2. **wheel arches and sills:** spheres around the four arch centres (material parameters set once on `M_Wrangler_Base` from spike 0), plus a low-height gradient, each arch driven by its own wheel;
    3. **doors and bumpers:** splatter, denser lower down;
    4. **hood, roof and glass:** sparse spots, plus a light film on the glass;
  - one dry mud colour and roughness, flat normals;
  - inputs: base colour, roughness, normal, the CPD values and the arch params; outputs the blended attributes.
- **CPD layout** (ADR-021):

  | Slot | Body | Tire |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | `BodyDirt` | its own wheel's dirt |
  | 1–4 | `WheelDirt[0..3]` | unused |

  The material tells body from tire by a static switch on the MI (`MI_Wrangler_Tire` = tire).
- **`PreviewDirt`** (−1 = off) on `UB1MudDirtComponent`:
  - when set, it writes every CPD value from `OnRegister` and `PostEditChangeProperty`, in the editor and in game;
  - the agent sets it through `ObjectTools` on a placed Wrangler, with no PIE and no console.
- **Photo booth:** a placed `BP_B1Jeep_Wrangler` on a clear, lit spot in `Lvl_Gym_Core` (folder `B1Tests/PhotoBooth`, away from the rigs). There are two saved camera transforms: front three-quarter left and rear three-quarter right (the two concept angles). An MCP script (`ProgrammaticToolset`) sets `PreviewDirt` to 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1, sets each camera, and captures with `CaptureViewport`.
- **User checkpoint 1:** the stage stills next to the concept images.

**B. Dirt accumulation**
- **`UB1MudDirtComponent`** (`Mud/`, portable, on `AB1Jeep` as `MudDirt`) reads only `UB1MudVehicleComponent`. It does **not** read the spray, so dirt keeps building with the spray off or absent.
  - **Thrown mud per wheel:** a static helper (`B1MudThrow`, `Mud/`) computes a 0..1 intensity from sink depth, tread speed and slip.
  - **Shared with the spray:** the spray uses the same helper for its rate, so what you see thrown matches what sticks.
  - **Wheels:** `WheelDirt[i]` grows at intensity / `WheelSecondsToFull`.
  - **Body:** `BodyDirt` grows at the mean intensity / `BodySecondsToFull`, plus a belly-contact term from deep mud.
  - **Authoring in seconds:** `WheelSecondsToFull` and `BodySecondsToFull` are the times to full at intensity 1 (category `Mud|Dirt`).
  - **Monotonic, capped at 1.** Nothing washes off. A respawn gives a fresh jeep.
- **Targets:** components tagged `B1MudDirt` (set on `BP_B1Jeep_Wrangler`).
  - At BeginPlay each one is matched to the nearest wheel (`IB1MudSimHost` wheel locations, within the wheel radius) → a tire for that wheel. A tagged component near no wheel is the body.
  - Logged once on `LogB1Mud` (`dirt targets: body=Chassis, w0=…`).
  - Writes CPD only when a value changed by ≥ 0.005.
- **Debug:**
  - a "Dirt" section in the Mud window: per-wheel and body bars, intensity, typed fields for both seconds-to-full values, Clean / Max buttons, and a live `PreviewDirt` field;
  - cheat `B1Dirt <0..1>` (negative = clean; user-side);
  - `dirt=` on the `B1Mud` telemetry;
  - a dirt line in `ShowDebug B1Mud`.
- **Saving the config** writes to the pawn's own BP class (`BP_B1Jeep_Wrangler` for the player), not `BP_B1Jeep`, because of the child-BP rule. This applies to the spray section too.
- **Rigs:**
  - the gym rig logs `dirtW=a/b/c/d dirtBody=` with `Min/MaxWheelDirt` and `Min/MaxBodyDirt` thresholds;
  - `StartDirt` (−1 = leave) sets the dirt at script start;
  - dirt rigs use `VehicleClass` = `BP_B1Jeep_Wrangler`.

**C. Spray rework (`NS_B1MudSpray`, `UB1MudSprayComponent`)**
- Three emitters in one system:
  - **Clumps:** GPU mesh particles (generated clump mesh), 2–12 cm with a skewed size distribution (many small, few big), random rotation and spin, gravity and drag. Depth-buffer collision: they stop on the ground, then shrink out.
  - **Droplets:** 0.5–3 cm velocity-aligned sprites.
  - **Haze:** a handful of large, soft, low-opacity brown sprites behind the wheel, slow, high drag.
- The clump material reuses the `M_B1MudSpray` look (masked, lit, wet), tinted by `User.Color`.
- **Rolling spray:** the rate comes from the `B1MudThrow` intensity, so a wheel rolling fast through mud throws mud as well as a spinning one, and a sunk wheel throws more.
- **User params:** keep `User.SpawnRate`, `User.SprayVelocity` and `User.Color`; add `User.Intensity` (0..1, scales the size and the haze opacity). The spawn point stays the current one (20 cm above the visible surface, behind the tread).
- **Debug:** a "Spray" section in the Mud window (per-wheel intensity and rate; typed fields for `Mud|Spray`; Save to the pawn's BP). CVar `b1.Mud.Spray` and `spray=` stay.
- **Spray capture:** a `bAutoRun` rig (`B1Spray_Medium`, full throttle from rest on the medium lane of `Lvl_Gym_Mud`, Wrangler), with the camera set before `StartPIE`, then `CaptureEditorImage`, next to frames from `mud-car-drive.mp4`.
- **User checkpoint 2:** a playtest in the Swamp.

**D. Docs**
- ADR-021, written into DECISIONS.md with this revision: a dirt material layer driven by CPD, not decals.
- MIGRATION.md:
  - `UB1MudDirtComponent` and `B1MudThrow`;
  - the `B1MudDirt` tag;
  - the CPD layout, plus `MF_B1MudDirt` and its textures, which the host material must call;
  - the spray's `User.Intensity` parameter.
- ARCHITECTURE.md via `/save`.

### Out of scope
- The `mud-car-splash.mp4` sheet splash, spray on the camera.
- Washing dirt off (Phase 3 can add a decrease path).
- Dirt driven by particle collisions, or painted into a UV render target.
- Dirt material on the buggy (`BP_B1Jeep`). The component runs on it, and it can call `MF_B1MudDirt` later.
- Dirt on the on-foot character, sound.
- Performance work (CLAUDE.md rule 4). Rates get capped, and that's all.

### Stretch
- **Fresh vs dried mud:** a `Wetness` value that decays over time (CPD slot 5), darker and glossier when fresh.
- **Tread-arc spawn:** particles start along the back-upper tire arc, if checkpoint 2 shows the single-point spawn reads wrong.

## Approach

Spray and dirt are game-thread visuals fed by the existing physics → game mud output (`UB1MudVehicleComponent`, ADR-003). Nothing touches the sim. One static helper, `B1MudThrow`, turns sink, tread speed and slip into a per-wheel "mud thrown" intensity. The spray turns it into a particle rate, and the dirt component turns it into monotonic 0..1 values, so the two agree but don't depend on each other. Dirt reaches the meshes as Custom Primitive Data on tagged components, matched to wheels by position: no dynamic material instances, and nothing project-specific in `Mud/`. The look lives in a mud-side material function, `MF_B1MudDirt`: a two-scale local-space triplanar mask with a region-weighted reveal threshold, called by `M_Wrangler_Base`. The look is built and approved first with `PreviewDirt` in an editor photo booth, then the accumulation, then the spray rebuild (three emitters, depth-buffer collision per ADR-011).

## Validation

**Agent (rigs + captures):**
- `B1Dirt_Firm`: 20 s at 50 % on firm ground → every dirt value is 0.
- `B1Dirt_Medium`: 15 s at 50 % across medium mud. Every `WheelDirt` ≥ 0.4, and `BodyDirt` < the minimum `WheelDirt` (tires lead).
- `B1Dirt_Drive90`: 90 s at 50 % in medium mud (the lane can be 50 m long, with the jeep reversing back and forth) → `BodyDirt` ≥ 0.9, and every `WheelDirt` = 1.
- `B1Dirt_Cap`: `StartDirt` 0.95, then 30 s of full throttle digging in → every value is exactly 1, with no change over the last 10 s.
- **Starting values:** these thresholds set the starting seconds-to-full values (wheel ~20 s, body ~80 s at intensity 1). If the user retunes, the thresholds follow the tunables.
- **No physics change:** the existing mud, fuel and winch suites stay unchanged.
- **Photo booth:** stills at dirt 0 / 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1 from both concept angles (checkpoint 1). Dirt 0 matches `jeep-wrangler.png`, and dirt 1 reads like `jeep_wrangler_mud_reference.jpg`.
- **Spray:** `B1Spray_Medium` captures next to frames from `mud-car-drive.mp4`. The `spray=` telemetry shows a non-zero rate when rolling with no slip (the rolling term).

**User (feel wins):**
- **Checkpoint 1 (stills):** the stages read as tires → arches → splatter → spots, and the cap matches the concept.
- **Checkpoint 2 (Swamp playtest):** the spray reads as mud (small clumps of mixed sizes, plus haze) on spin and when rolling. The dirt builds and stops at about the concept look in about 90 s of mud driving.
- The user signs off.

## Risks

1. **The look itself.** A generated triplanar mask may not reach the concept look. Mitigations:
   - look-first ordering, so the logic isn't tuned around a look that changes;
   - two-scale breakup;
   - comparable captures from fixed poses;
   - if it still reads fake, Fab textures drop into the same `MF_B1MudDirt` inputs.
2. **Tire ↔ wheel mapping.** Matching by position depends on the tires sitting at their wheel locations at BeginPlay (they're attached to the `VisWheel_*` sockets). Mitigations:
   - the one-time `dirt targets:` log;
   - a rig check that four tires and one body were found.
   Fallback: per-wheel tags (`B1MudDirt.Wheel0..3`).
3. **CPD on inherited C++ components.** The tags or the CPD may not be editable on the inherited tire components in the BP. Mitigations:
   - spike 0;
   - fallback: the host sets the tags in `AB1Jeep`'s constructor (host-side, so portability holds).
4. **The Niagara toolset can't build everything** (mesh renderer, collision, user params). Mitigations:
   - spike 0;
   - fallback: clump sprites with a mask;
   - if a module can't be wired, the user does it from the agent's written spec.
5. **Rolling spray floods particles at speed, and haze overdraw.** Mitigations: per-wheel rate cap, a handful of haze sprites, a `stat Niagara` look (tuning only).
6. **Rig noise.** Dirt integrates over noisy slip. Mitigations: thresholds check ordering and the cap, not exact values.

## Critique (round 1) — 2026-11-07

Checked against the live MCP server: `list_toolsets` shows `NiagaraToolset_System` (systems, emitters, modules, data access), `MaterialTools`, and `TextureTools.import_file` (PNG → Texture2D). OBJ mesh import already works (memory note).

### Architectural alignment — 4/5
The plan fits. Spray and dirt stay game-thread visuals fed by the existing physics → game mud output (ADR-003). Nothing touches the sim, depth-buffer collision is kept (ADR-011), and the look stays in a BP/material variant (ADR-020). The host opts in through a tag, per the portability rules.

Three things don't fit cleanly:
1. **Dirt reads the spray component's spawn rates.** That couples two visuals: `b1.Mud.Spray 0`, or a host without the spray, would stop body dirt. Dirt should compute its own "thrown mud" term from `UB1MudVehicleComponent`, through a shared static helper if the formula should match.
2. **The dirt layer is drawn inline in `M_Wrangler_Base`.** That's a project asset, so the dirt look can't migrate with `Mud/` and the buggy can't reuse it. It belongs in a material function (`MF_B1MudDirt`, next to the mud assets) that `M_Wrangler_Base` calls. MIGRATION.md should list it with the CPD layout.
3. **"Save to BP archetype" for the spray config.** It hits the child-BP rule: edits to `BP_B1Jeep` don't reach `BP_B1Jeep_Wrangler`, which is what the player drives. The save must target the pawn's own BP class (mcp-tools.md, child BP CDOs).

### Scope clarity — 3/5
The out-of-scope list is good: no splash sheet, no washing, no UV painting, no buggy material. Two items will creep:
- **"Darker/glossier when fresh"** implies a wetness state that decays over time. That's a second simulated value nobody specified. Cut it to a stretch goal, or define it (a `Wetness` value that decays, a separate CPD slot).
- **"Spawn on the tread arc"**, plus a rolling term, plus three emitters, plus new user params, is a full spray redesign. The plan doesn't say which of these the reference actually needs. The drive video's tail comes off the rear of the tire at contact height and above. An arc is nice, not essential.

The sentence "new user parameters as needed" is open-ended: list them.

This plan also covers Phase 1's stretch item "dirt mask on the jeep". The roadmap should say so.

### Validation specificity — 3/5
The rig checks are concrete: firm = 0, medium has the wheels ahead of the body, the cap is reached and held. The success sentence and the rigs disagree, though. The goal says "a minute or two of driving through medium mud", but the cap rig digs in at full throttle for 90 s, which is a different input. There should be a time-to-cap target under normal driving: for example, 50 % throttle through the Swamp for ~90 s gives body ≥ 0.9.

The rates should be authored as times, not raw gains:
- `WheelSecondsToFull` / `BodySecondsToFull` at a reference intensity;
- for a given rig, the thresholds then follow from the tunables, and the user can tune in seconds.

The look checks ("next to the concept images") need fixed camera poses that match the two concept angles: front three-quarter left, rear three-quarter right. Without them, captures from one iteration to the next aren't comparable. The spray needs a reproducible spin: a `bAutoRun` rig on a medium lane, full throttle from rest, with the camera set before `StartPIE` (mcp-tools.md).

### Risk realism — 2/5
Risk 3 (texture import) is gone: `TextureTools.import_file` exists. The Niagara fallback in Dependencies is moot too. The real top risks are missing:
1. **Wheel ↔ tire mapping.** One `B1MudDirt` tag doesn't tell the dirt component which tire belongs to which wheel index. Without that, per-wheel tire dirt can't be done. Options:
   - per-wheel tags (`B1MudDirt.Wheel0..3`);
   - matching each tagged component to the nearest wheel at BeginPlay;
   - an `IB1MudSimHost` method.
   Matching to the nearest wheel needs no host work, and each component's position is a fixed socket.
2. **The agent can't use the cheat.** The memory note says MCP has no console exec, so `B1Dirt` is user-only. The agent's dirt-stage captures need a property path:
   - a `PreviewDirt` (−1 = off) on the component, applied in `OnRegister` / `PostEditChangeProperty`, so a Wrangler placed in an editor level shows any stage with no PIE;
   - plus a rig field `StartDirt`.
   This is the difference between the agent iterating on the look alone and needing the user every round.
3. **The look itself is the schedule risk.** Hitting the concept with a generated triplanar mask is the uncertain part, not the C++. The plan orders work spray → dirt logic → look; it should be look-first:
   - build `MF_B1MudDirt` and step through it with `PreviewDirt` against the concept captures;
   - get the user's OK on stills;
   - only then build the accumulation.
   Otherwise the logic gets built and tuned around a look that may change.

Risks 1, 2, 5 and 6 are real but minor. The CPD default of 0 is the engine default, so risk 2 shrinks to "check that the CPD slots aren't already used by the Wrangler MIs".

### Agent ergonomics — 3/5
Good:
- the rig metrics (`dirtW=` / `dirtBody=`) chain into the existing `B1Test` line and `RunGymTests.ps1`;
- `dirt=` on telemetry;
- ImGui for the user.

Gaps:
- the cheat-only stage control (above);
- the plan has no `PreviewDirt` / `StartDirt` path, and no capture rig or scene for the two concept poses.

A small photo-booth setup would make every look iteration one MCP script (`ProgrammaticToolset`):
- a spot in `Lvl_Gym_Core` with a placed `BP_B1Jeep_Wrangler` and two saved camera transforms;
- for each step: set `PreviewDirt`, set the camera, capture.

Generated textures should be re-runnable with fixed seeds and documented parameters, so an agent can regenerate a variant without re-deriving the pipeline.

### Implementability — 3/5
This is a sprint, but a full one. There are two independent streams:
- **Dirt:** generator, material function, four stages, component, CPD, wheel mapping, debug, rigs, ADR, MIGRATION.
- **Spray:** clump mesh, three emitters through the Niagara toolset, rolling term, ImGui.

Each needs its own user look-check. It fits as one sprint (Sprint 5) if it's split into small tasks with two checkpoints, and if the spikes go first:
- MCP builds a GPU mesh-renderer emitter with depth-buffer collision;
- CPD on the C++ tire components (are `ComponentTags` editable on the inherited components in `BP_B1Jeep_Wrangler`?);
- importing one generated PNG.

If the Niagara toolset can't set up a mesh renderer with collision, the clump emitter falls back to sprites with a clump-shaped mask. That's not a stop.

### Recommended changes
1. **Decouple dirt from spray:** dirt computes its own thrown-mud term from `UB1MudVehicleComponent`, with a shared helper if needed.
2. **Material function `MF_B1MudDirt`** (mud-side asset, listed in MIGRATION.md) called by `M_Wrangler_Base`, not an inline graph. The CPD slot layout is documented in ADR-021.
3. **Wheel mapping:** match tagged components to the nearest wheel at BeginPlay; the body = the tagged component that isn't near any wheel, or a second tag. Add it as risk 1.
4. **Agent-reachable dirt control:** a `PreviewDirt` property (editor-visible, no PIE) and a rig `StartDirt`. The `B1Dirt` cheat stays for the user.
5. **Look-first ordering:**
   - spikes: Niagara mesh renderer + collision; CPD on the inherited tire components; PNG import;
   - then the generator → `MF_B1MudDirt` → stage stills at the two concept poses → **user checkpoint 1** (stills);
   - then accumulation + rigs;
   - then the spray rework → **user checkpoint 2** (playtest).
6. **Authoring in seconds:** `WheelSecondsToFull` / `BodySecondsToFull` at a reference intensity. Add a normal-driving time-to-cap target (50 % through medium, body ≥ 0.9 in ~90 s) alongside the cap rig.
7. **Trim the spray scope:** keep the three emitters and the rolling term. Make the tread-arc spawn a stretch unless the first capture shows the single-point spawn reads wrong. List the exact new user params.
8. **Cut "fresh vs dried" wetness to a stretch goal,** or define its state explicitly.
9. **Fix the stale risks:** drop risk 3 and the Niagara dependency fallback, and add the three real risks above.
10. **Spray config save:** target the pawn's own BP class (child-BP rule).
11. **Photo-booth scene:** a placed Wrangler plus two camera transforms matching the concept angles, used for every capture.
12. **ROADMAP:** Phase V absorbs Phase 1's "dirt mask on the jeep" stretch item.
