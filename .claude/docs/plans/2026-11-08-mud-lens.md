# PLAN — Mud on the lens

Status: revised (revision 1, 2026-11-08). Next: `/plan-roadmap`. Lands in Phase V (Sprint 5) before checkpoint 2 (S5-19), at the user's request.

## Problem

Big mud throws from the tires should hit the player's view. The chase camera's lens gets dirty when the tires throw a large spray towards it, the mud fades after a while, and new splashes build on what's already there (like the tire coat). The maximum is `Concept/mud-on-the-screen.jpg`: hundreds of small dark specks plus a few dozen larger muddy drops that refract the scene, spread over the whole screen, with the HUD clean on top.

The trigger is a cheap guess from the spray's own throw (launch speed, direction, intensity), not particles hitting the camera. The user wants it generous: the camera gets splashed more often than not while the wheels throw hard, even when it's a bit outside the spray's sideways spread.

## Scope

- **Trigger.** Per wheel, an *exposure* 0..1 = throw intensity × throw strength (a launch-speed band) × wide aim × distance falloff. Exposure = max over the wheels. Only the active camera tagged `B1MudLens` counts.
- **Accumulation.** K = 4 splash slots. A splash fills an empty slot with a random screen offset and one of 8 rotation/mirror variants, or tops up the weakest slot. `LensDirt` = mean slot strength (1 = the concept image) and grows at exposure / `LensSecondsToFull` on average. Each slot holds after its last hit, then fades. Seeded random, so rigs repeat.
- **Look.** A post-process material `M_B1LensMud` on the tagged camera: specks plus refracting drops, revealed by slot strength. Generated textures from `Scripts/MudTextures`.
- **Debug.** Mud window "Lens" section, cheats, telemetry, ShowDebug line, `PreviewLensDirt`.
- **Tests.** Rig metrics and four lens rigs (measure first, then thresholds), editor-world look stills, one PIE confirmation capture, then a playtest inside S5-19.

### Out of scope

- Particle-to-lens hits, a render target painted by real impacts.
- Occlusion by the body (a front wheel's throw counts through the jeep). Revisit after the playtest if it looks wrong.
- In-cabin (front) camera. The windshield already gets glass dirt (ADR-021). The front camera isn't tagged.
- Wipers, washing, water on the lens (Phase 3 can reuse the slots for river splashes).
- On-foot camera (a parked jeep doesn't spray anyway).
- Spray from other vehicles, mud thrown by impacts or landings.
- Performance work (CLAUDE.md §4). One full-screen pass with ~10 texture samples is acceptable.

### Stretch

- Drops running down the lens as they age (a per-slot UV slide).
- Splashes centred on the throwing wheel's screen position (a per-slot radial mask).

## Approach

### Where it lives (portable, `Mud/`)

`UB1MudLensComponent` (`Mud/B1MudLensComponent`) on `AB1Jeep` as `MudLens`, next to `MudSpray` and `MudDirt`.
- Finds `UB1MudSprayComponent` on its owner and camera components tagged `B1MudLens` (host tags `BackCamera` in `AB1Jeep`'s constructor). Only a camera with `IsActive()` accumulates and shows mud. `DoToggleCamera` turns the back camera off, so its mud stays hidden and keeps fading.
- Creates one MID of `UB1MudSettings::LensMaterial` (new soft ref, default `/Game/B1/FX/MI_B1LensMud`). Appends it to the camera's `PostProcessSettings.WeightedBlendables` (append, don't overwrite; removed in EndPlay). Writes slot params to the MID when they change.
- Reads launch data from the spray component, so spray off (`b1.Mud.Spray 0`) means a clean lens. That's intended: the lens shows the visible spray. Dirt (ADR-021) stays independent of the spray.

The spray component gets read-only getters for what it already computes per wheel: `GetWheelSprayOrigin`, `GetWheelSprayVelocity` (world, cm/s; zero when not spraying). No change to spray behaviour.

### Exposure (per wheel, every tick)

From the emitter origin O, the launch velocity v (speed s = |v|), the camera position C and d = |C − O|:
- **Throw strength.** smoothstep(`LensMinThrowSpeed`, `LensFullThrowSpeed`, s), start values 350 / 500 cm/s.
  - Today's numbers: launch speed = 250 + slip(cm/s) × 0.6.
  - A rolling throw (~250 cm/s) gives 0.
  - Full throttle in medium (slip 4.2–5.0 m/s → ~510 cm/s, from the last PIE log) gives 1.
  - It follows spray retuning in the right direction (faster spray → more lens mud).
  - The rig task measures rolling, medium-spin and deep-spin launch speeds before the defaults are fixed.
- **Aim (wide).** Horizontal angle α between v and C − O.
  - 1 while α ≤ `LensAimFull` (75°), smoothstep down to 0 at `LensAimZero` (110°).
  - With the chase camera behind, forward wheelspin counts even when the camera is orbited well off-axis.
  - Only a camera swung round to the front, or reverse spin throwing away from it, stays clean.
- **Distance.** `1 / (1 + (d / LensFalloffDistance)²)`, `LensFalloffDistance` 8 m (≈ 0.68 at the chase camera's ~5.5 m).
- **Intensity.** `UB1MudSprayComponent::GetWheelIntensity` (`B1MudThrow`).

Exposure E = max over the wheels.

### Splashes and slots

- While E > `LensSplashThreshold` (0.1), a splash fires every `LensSplashInterval` (0.25 s, ±40 %, from a per-component `FRandomStream` seeded at BeginPlay).
- Amount = interval × E × K / `LensSecondsToFull` (start 4 s). On average, `LensDirt` (mean of K slots) rises at E / `LensSecondsToFull`: 4 s of full exposure gives the concept image.
- Slot = (strength, offset UV, variant 0–7, time since last hit). A splash takes the emptiest slot (< 0.05) with new random placement, otherwise it adds to the weakest slot without moving it. Strength caps at 1.
- Per slot: no change for `LensHoldSeconds` (1.5 s) after its last hit, then a linear drop to 0 over `LensFadeSeconds` (6 s). Start values, tuned at the playtest.
- `PreviewLensDirt` (−1 off) sets every slot for captures. `SetLensDirt(v)` / `CleanLens()` / `Splash()` for debug.

### Look (`M_B1LensMud` + `MI_B1LensMud`)

- Post-process, blendable location **after tonemapping**: drops don't go through TSR or bloom, and the UMG HUD stays clean on top, as in the concept.
- Slot inputs are MI params `LensSlot0..3` (vector: strength, offset U, offset V, variant). The component's MID writes them; a preview MI sets them by hand.
- Generated textures (`gen_mud_textures.py`, fixed seed), **2048×1024**, so one tile covers a 16:9 screen without repeating:
  - `T_B1LensDrops`: RG = drop normal xy, B = dome height, A = per-drop reveal id (histogram-equalized, so threshold t reveals fraction t of the drops). Sizes skewed small, a few large ones, as in the concept.
  - `T_B1LensSpecks`: R = speck mask, G = per-speck reveal id. Many 2–8 px specks, some clustered.
  - Each slot's field is a quarter of the concept density, so 4 full slots = the concept.
- Per slot (UV = screen UV with aspect, then variant rotation/mirror and offset), in one Custom node like `MF_B1MudDirt`:
  - **Specks:** shown if id < strength, alpha fades with strength, colour `LensSpeckColor` (dark brown).
  - **Drops:** shown if id < strength, and the drop shrinks as strength falls (dome height > 1 − strength × `LensDropFill`). The scene is sampled at UV − normal × `LensRefraction` (the flipped look of a lens drop), tinted towards `LensDropTint`, with a darker rim from the normal length and a small highlight.
- All 4 slots in one pass: 8 field samples + up to 4 scene samples. All slots at 0 returns the scene unchanged (all lerps alpha 0, early-out).

### Look loop (agent)

- **Stills without PIE.** A bound `PostProcessVolume` `B1PhotoBooth_LensPreview` in `Lvl_Gym_Core` (folder `B1Tests/PhotoBooth`), around a new chase pose behind the booth Wrangler, with `MI_B1LensMud_Preview` (slot params set by hand). Saved disabled; the script enables it, sets the slot strengths for each level, captures with `CaptureViewport` and disables it again. `photobooth.py` / `stage_sheet.py` gain a lens mode that puts the stills next to `Concept/mud-on-the-screen.jpg`.
- **One PIE confirmation** at the end: `PreviewLensDirt` 1 on the player's jeep, `CaptureEditorImage`, to prove the camera blendable path (and the clean HUD on top).

### Debug (imgui.md rules)

- Mud window → "Lens":
  - `LensDirt` bar and a slot table (strength, hold/fade state, offset, variant);
  - per-wheel exposure with its four factors (throw, aim, distance, intensity), plus launch speed and angle α;
  - splash count;
  - typed fields for every tunable, buttons Splash / Clean / Max;
  - "Save to asset" on the pawn BP's component archetype (same path as Dirt and Spray).
- The material's look params live on `MI_B1LensMud`, edited in the editor.
- Cheats `B1Lens <v>` (negative = clean) and `B1LensSplash`.
- Telemetry `lens=<dirt>/<exposure>` on the `B1Mud` line. ShowDebug B1Mud: `lens d  exp e  slots a/b/c/d`.
- CVar `b1.Mud.Lens`.

### Order (look first, like Sprint 5)

1. Spray getters + lens component (exposure, slots, fade, preview) + setting/tag + build.
2. Texture generator + `M_B1LensMud` / `MI_B1LensMud`; preview volume stills at 0 / 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1 next to the concept → **lens checkpoint** (user signs off the look). One PIE confirmation capture.
3. Debug section, cheats, telemetry.
4. Rig metrics + rigs: measure, then set thresholds and tune defaults.
5. Docs (ADR-022, MIGRATION.md, ARCHITECTURE.md via `/save`), then the S5-19 playtest covers the lens too.

## Validation

- **Rigs** (`Lvl_Gym_Mud`, folder `B1Tests/Lens`, Wrangler). The rig's jeep has no view, but its `BackCamera` is active, so exposure runs headless; the task checks this first.
  - Line fields `lensPeak= lensEnd= lensSplashes= lensExpPeak= throwPeak=` (max launch speed, cm/s).
  - Thresholds `Min/MaxLensPeak`, `MaxLensEnd`, `Min/MaxLensSplashes`, `MaxLensExposure`.
  - **Measure first.** Run Firm, Roll and Spin with no thresholds and record `throwPeak` / `lensExpPeak`. Then set the throw-speed defaults and the thresholds below. If Roll splashes, tune the defaults, not the thresholds.
  - `B1Lens_Firm`: 20 s at 50 % on firm → 0 splashes, exposure 0.
  - `B1Lens_Roll`: 50 % cross of medium (rolling throw) → peak ≤ 0.1 and below Spin's.
  - `B1Lens_Spin`: 4 s full throttle from rest in medium (DigInMedium spot) → ≥ 3 splashes, peak ≥ 0.3. A second run with `StartLensDirt` 0.9 never shows > 1 (cap).
  - `B1Lens_Fade`: spin 4 s, then 10 s idle → end ≤ 0.05 (hold + fade = 7.5 s).
- Existing suites unchanged: core 12, mud 28, winch 36.
- **Look:** preview stills at 5 levels vs `Concept/mud-on-the-screen.jpg`, user sign-off. A clean capture (all slots 0) is identical to the volume disabled. The PIE capture shows the mud under a clean HUD.
- **Feel (S5-19):** flooring it in medium splashes the lens within ~1 s and fills it to about the concept in a few seconds of wheelspin. Rolling through mud doesn't splash it. A side orbit still gets splashed, while a front view doesn't. It clears within ~8 s of the last splash. Feel wins over the rig thresholds.

## Risks

1. **The exposure model doesn't match the visible spray.** The first draft's ballistic reach would never have fired: medium wheelspin launches at ~510 cm/s and couldn't reach a camera 5.5 m back. The speed band replaces it, but its edges are still guesses. Mitigation: measure launch speeds in the rigs before fixing the defaults. The Lens section shows all four factors live, and every edge is a typed tunable.
2. **Spray retuning in S5-19 moves the lens trigger.** The user is tuning the spray now; a different `SpraySpeedPerSlip` or `MinSpraySpeed` shifts launch speeds. Mitigation: the band moves the right way, and its edges sit in the same Mud window next to the spray fields. Re-run the lens rigs after any spray save.
3. **Drops read as rain, not mud.** Mitigation: speck-heavy mix, brown drop tint, look checkpoint before the trigger work. The ratio of specks to drops and the tint are MI params.
4. **Visible repetition** (same pattern every splash). Mitigation: 2:1 texture with no on-screen repeat, random offset + 8 variants per slot, 4 overlapping slots; check stills at 0.5 with different seeds.
5. **The preview path differs from the game path.** Volume PP and camera PP blend the same material, but the camera path could be overwritten by settings on `BackCamera` (template, BP). Mitigation: append to `WeightedBlendables`, check the camera's PP is empty first, and confirm with one PIE capture (`CaptureEditorImage`, camera set before `StartPIE`, at most 2 captures per reply, scalability medium / shadows high).

## Dependencies

- Spray rework done (S5-13..S5-16): intensity, launch velocity, spread params.
- Phase V is still open: S5-19 (checkpoint 2) waits for this plan's tasks.
- Photo booth in `Lvl_Gym_Core` and `photobooth.py` / `stage_sheet.py` (S5-08).

Alternatives: UMG overlay (no refraction), Niagara sprites on the near plane, impact-painted render target, a ballistic reach test (doesn't fire at real spray speeds). No conflict with ADR-011 (one PP pass, nothing ray traced) or ADR-021 (dirt stays independent of the spray; the lens reads it on purpose).

## Critique (round 1) — 2026-11-08

User feedback taken in: **aim should be wide.** The chase camera should get splashed more often than not, even when it's a bit outside the spray's sideways spread.

### Architectural alignment — 4/5
It fits the project's patterns well. One portable component in `Mud/` reaches the host through a tag (`B1MudLens`, the same pattern as `B1MudDirt`), a soft ref in `UB1MudSettings`, and read-only getters on another `Mud/` component. It has no project includes, sits next to `MudSpray`/`MudDirt` on `AB1Jeep`, and its debug follows imgui.md. Reading the spray on purpose, unlike dirt (ADR-021), is argued and goes into ADR-022. A camera PP blendable is the cheapest way to get refraction and costs one pass (ADR-011 is fine). Weak spot: the optional occlusion trace ("ignoring the owner except its body") is vague, and it may not do anything. `Chassis` is a mesh on the pawn, and whether it blocks Visibility is unchecked. A trace from just behind the tire also risks hitting the tire itself. It adds a channel decision to a portable class for an unproven gain.

### Scope clarity — 4/5
The in/out boundary is sharp: chase camera only, no impact painting, no wipers or water, no other vehicles. Two places could creep: the occlusion trace (above), and the per-slot age/offset logic, which could invite "drops run down the lens" or "splash centred on the wheel's screen position" mid-task. Neither is listed, so name them as out of scope or a stretch. The front camera "keeps fading while hidden" is a good, cheap rule.

### Validation specificity — 3/5
The rigs pin the right order: firm < roll < spin, then fade and cap. But the thresholds are guessed before anything is measured. `B1Lens_Roll` "≤ 1 splash" depends on the slip at 50 % in medium, which nobody has measured with the Wrangler. Splash timing also uses random intervals with no seed, so rig counts can wobble. The accumulation speed is buried in three numbers (interval 0.25 s, amount 0.35 × E, mean of 4 slots). Worked out, exposure 1 fills the lens to the concept in about 3 s of wheelspin, and the plan never says so. The project's convention is seconds-to-full (`WheelSecondsToFull`, `TireCoatSecondsToFull`), and the lens should follow it so the rig and the playtest talk in the same unit. The look criterion ("stills vs the concept, user sign-off") is fine.

### Risk realism — 2/5
The real top risk is missing. **The reach gate doesn't fire at today's spray speeds.** The last PIE log (`Saved/Logs/MudDriver.log`, full throttle in medium, Wrangler) shows slip 4.2–5.0 m/s. That gives a launch speed of 250 + 430 × 0.6 ≈ 510 cm/s. The fastest particle (×1.35) is ~690 cm/s, with a safety parabola peak of ~2.4 m and **no reach at 5.5 m back**. The "900 cm/s full wheelspin" in the plan needs ~10.8 m/s of slip, which only an unloaded wheel gets. S5-17 saw the same thing from the other side: "a low, flat trail at wheel height". So the main case, flooring it in mud, would never splash the lens. A true ballistic gate also changes whenever the user retunes the spray in S5-19, which is happening right now. Risks 1–5 as written are real but secondary. Risk 1 (trigger vs what the player sees) comes closest, but blames tuning rather than the model.

### Agent ergonomics — 3/5
The debug surface is good: exposure factors in the Lens section, telemetry `lens=`, cheats, `PreviewLensDirt`, and rig fields that chain into the existing `B1Test` line. The look loop is slow, though. PIE + `CaptureEditorImage`, at most 2 captures per reply, a PIE start per stage and an editor-window crop make 5 levels × iterations costly. A cheaper loop: an unbound `PostProcessVolume` with a preview MI (slot strengths and placements as MI params) in `Lvl_Gym_Core` near the photo booth. `CaptureViewport` renders editor-world PP volumes, so stills need no PIE. Keep one PIE capture at the end to confirm the camera blendable path. Risk 5 should name this instead of accepting the slow path.

### Implementability — 4/5
About 6 tasks, ~12–15 h: component + getters 3 h, generator + material 4 h, stills/checkpoint 1.5 h, debug 2 h, rigs 2.5 h, docs 1 h. That fits the rest of Sprint 5 without splitting. The material is the biggest unknown (refraction + 4 slots × UV transforms in a Custom node, as `MF_B1MudDirt` did), but there's a precedent. Dropping the occlusion trace and the ballistic maths makes the component simpler.

### Recommended changes
1. **Replace the ballistic reach gate with a throw-strength band.** Throw = smoothstep(`LensMinThrowSpeed`, `LensFullThrowSpeed`, launch speed), starting at 350 / 500 cm/s, so a rolling throw (~250) gives 0 and medium wheelspin (~510) gives 1. Multiply by a distance falloff from emitter to camera (`LensFalloffDistance`, start 8 m). Measure the real launch speeds (rolling, medium spin, deep spin) in a rig before fixing the defaults. It stays tied to the visible spray, follows spray retuning in the right direction, and needs no physics argument.
2. **Wide aim (user feedback).** Aim = 1 while the horizontal angle between the throw and emitter → camera is within `LensAimFull` (start 75°), falling to 0 at `LensAimZero` (start 110°). With the chase camera behind, forward wheelspin always counts, even orbited well off-axis. Only a camera swung round to the front, or reverse spin throwing away from it, stays clean.
3. **Drop the occlusion trace** from the plan (front-wheel throws count through the body; revisit after the playtest if it looks wrong). Exposure = max over the wheels.
4. **State accumulation in seconds:** `LensSecondsToFull` (start 4 s at exposure 1). Derive the splash amount as interval × exposure / `LensSecondsToFull` × K, so `LensDirt` grows at exposure / `LensSecondsToFull` on average. Keep `LensHoldSeconds` / `LensFadeSeconds`. Use a seeded `FRandomStream` per component so rig counts repeat.
5. **Record first, then set thresholds.** The lens rig task first logs `lensExpPeak` / launch speeds for Firm, Roll and Spin, then sets thresholds on the order (Firm 0, Roll < Spin, Spin peak ≥ 0.3, Fade end ≤ 0.05). Tune defaults, not thresholds, if Roll splashes.
6. **Look loop without PIE:** a preview MI on an unbound PPV for the stills, with one PIE `CaptureEditorImage` to confirm the camera blendable. Rewrite risk 5 to match.
7. **Name drips and wheel-centred splashes** as stretch (or out of scope), so they don't creep into the material task.
8. **Rewrite risk 1** as "exposure model doesn't match the visible spray" with the measured numbers above, and add "spray retuning in S5-19 moves the lens trigger": mitigated by the speed band being tunable in the same Lens section.
